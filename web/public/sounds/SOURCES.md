# Conch interface sounds

message.wav, join.wav, leave.wav and share.wav are project-made sounds,
created for Conch using the adjacent generate.py. No recordings, samples,
third-party audio or external tools are used. Each is a short sequence of
sine tones with a smooth envelope, mono PCM at 24 kHz.

The four WAV files and the generator are part of Conch, under the project's
licence (AGPL-3.0-only). Regenerate with `python3 generate.py`.
